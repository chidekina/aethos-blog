## Editing rules that bit this session

🔴 **Never edit a `.sh` while it is running.**

🔴 **A programmatic patch asserts its anchor before writing and checks the EFFECT
after.** `str.replace()` with a wrong anchor raises nothing and rewrites the file
identically. Four anchors broke this session because *I* moved the lines they
targeted; the harnesses reported `ANCHOR MISSED` rather than green, which is the
only reason those mutations were not silently retired.

🔴 **Enumerate patch sites by CONCEPT, never by the literal you remember.** Three
test arms needed `--llm`; grepping the string I had in mind found two of them. The
concept was "every invocation that sets `OLLAMA_URL`".

<decisions_made>
- **Summarisation is opt-in; translation stays.** This started as one flag and it
  shipped a real defect: with the model off, `summaryPt = summaryEn`, and **94 of
  94 PT lines came out byte-identical to their EN lines** — the Portuguese edition
  of a bilingual blog, in English. §3, §3b and §3c all missed it because all three
  compared EN line quality only. The measurements were sound; the question was
  narrow. The measured case against the model is entirely about summarisation;
  there is no record against translation, and removing it breaks the deliverable.

- **The two halves fail differently, on purpose.** `--llm-summary` with no model
  exits 2 — you asked for it, and quietly handing back excerpts is the opposite.
  Translation alone degrades and says so, because a weekly draft in one language
  beats no draft but a *quietly* monolingual one reads as translated.
  `TRANSLATED n/m` prints on every run, including the good one.

- **`--llm` and `--no-llm` keep their old both-on / both-off meanings**, so every
  existing caller keeps working. Dropping `--no-llm` from `KNOWN_FLAGS` would turn
  those callers into exit 2 — a broken instrument, not a verdict. A mutation
  asserts it.

- **`Today is … day .` is NOT stripped, and the reason is a cost.** It occurs
  **1 in 94** — first measured at **0**, from a predicate written
  `Today is[^.]{0,60}day` whose negated class stopped at the dot in a version
  number. With a working predicate the rule also eats `Today is a good day to
  ship, and the release notes are long.` One excerpt against a false positive on
  ordinary prose is a bad trade.

- **`enforce_admins` stays OFF on `main`**, so the docs-only carve-out survives.
  The cost is written down in the repo's `CLAUDE.md`: code on `main` is held by
  the local hook only. Restoration command included, verified by read-back.

- **Edition 2's 5-of-8 survival rate is recorded even though it argues against the
  flag flip.** Edition 1's figure was zero and is half of what §3d rests on. n=1
  against n=1, so not reversed — but a document that argues only from the half of
  the evidence agreeing with it is the defect this whole repo is about.
